✨ Foretold: The Promise of Messiah's Birth

A shared study for Chris & Kimberly — walking through the promise of the Messiah's coming, spoken by God hundreds of years before Bethlehem.

✨ The Promise Before the Person

Long before a manger in Bethlehem, before shepherds or a star, God was already speaking. Across centuries — through Moses, David, Isaiah, Micah, and more — He laid down promise after promise about a coming One.

Below, the two of us study it together — each entry stamped with a name and the moment it was written.

📜 The Prophecies — Chronological

We walk these in the order God gave them across history — from the Garden forward to Bethlehem. More will be added as our study grows.

🔗 Key Prophecies of the Birth
• Genesis 3:15 — the seed of the woman
• Isaiah 7:14 — a virgin will conceive; Immanuel
• Isaiah 9:6–7 — "For a child will be born to us"
• Micah 5:2 — born in Bethlehem, "from the days of eternity"

We will read each promise verbatim from the NASB 1995, ask what it meant to those who first heard it, and trace how it was kept in Christ — letting the text say what it says (2 Timothy 3:16–17).
