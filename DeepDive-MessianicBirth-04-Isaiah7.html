Prophecy 04 · The Sign of the Virgin

A Virgin Will Be with Child

Isaiah 7:14

Now the promise takes on flesh and detail. Speaking to a faithless King Ahaz, God offers a sign, one so staggering it reaches centuries past Ahaz's day. A virgin would bear a son, and His name would be Immanuel, "God with us." For the first time, the manner of the birth itself becomes the miracle.

Why this matters for the birth of Messiah
"A virgin will be with child": The birth itself is now the miracle — not merely the lineage, but the manner.
"Immanuel — God with us": The name declares the child's identity: God, dwelling among His people.
Matthew's citation: Matthew 1:22–23 applies this directly to the birth of Jesus — a link we'll examine, including the near-and-far layers of the sign.

Questions for our study: How did this sign speak to Ahaz in his own moment, and how does Matthew read it as fulfilled in Jesus? What do we make of a prophecy with both a near horizon and a far one? We let the text govern (2 Timothy 3:16–17).
