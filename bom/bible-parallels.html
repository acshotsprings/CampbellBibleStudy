Why this matters for the Deep Dive's argument: The Book of Mormon claims to be a translation of an ancient record written by Hebrew migrants who left Jerusalem around 600 BC. Many of the Bible parallels documented here raise chronology questions:
  • NT echoes — How do BoM authors writing centuries BC quote distinctively Pauline (1 Corinthians 13) or Johannine (John 10) phrasing?
  • Malachi 4:1 echoes in 1 Nephi 22 and 2 Nephi 26 — Both written c. 570-559 BC. Malachi wasn't written until c. 430 BC.
  • Direct verbatim quotation of KJV English — Including KJV translators' italicized supplied words that aren't in the Hebrew. How does an ancient text translated by divine power match 17th-century English translation choices?
