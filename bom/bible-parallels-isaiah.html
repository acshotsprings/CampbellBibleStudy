Why Isaiah matters for the Deep Dive's argument:
  • It's the largest single Bible block in the BoM — 19 chapters, 388 verses. No other prophet is quoted at this scale.
  • It matches the KJV verbatim — including italicized words KJV translators added that weren't in the Hebrew. If the BoM is a translation from ancient Hebrew plates, why does it match the KJV's 17th-century English supplemental words?
  • Including "Deutero-Isaiah" (chapters 40+) — most modern scholars hold that Isaiah 40–66 was written after the Babylonian exile (~540 BC). But the BoM includes Isaiah 48–54 as having been known to Nephi (who left Jerusalem c. 600 BC), 60+ years BEFORE Deutero-Isaiah was supposedly written.
  • Isaiah 53 in Mosiah 14 — Abinadi reads this Suffering Servant chapter to King Noah's court ~150 BC, ~115 years before Christ's actual Passion the chapter is read as prophesying.
