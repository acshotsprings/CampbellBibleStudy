The Book of Mormon (1830 Edition)

"A reproduction of the original as printed in 1830 by E. B. Grandin, in Palmyra, NY."

⚠️ OCR DISCLAIMER: The text on these pages was OCR'd from a scanned facsimile of the 1830 printing (Internet Archive: Jenson 2012 digital replica). Expect scattered scan errors throughout — "I, Neput" for "I, Nephi", "Anp" for "And", "L," for "I," at line starts, etc. These artifacts are NOT changes — they're noise from the scanning process. The original 1830 spelling and grammar (which are themselves often nonstandard) have been preserved on top of this OCR layer.
Why this matters — the four most important 1830 readings, verified in this data:
  • 1 Nephi Ch 3: "the virgin which thou seest, is the mother of God" — modern edition reads "mother of the Son of God" (1837 insertion). Read it →
  • 1 Nephi Ch 3: "behold the Lamb of God, yea, even the Eternal Father" — modern reads "the Son of the Eternal Father" (1837 insertion).
  • 2 Nephi Ch 12: "they shall be a white and a delightsome people" — modern (1981) reads "pure and delightsome." Read it →
  • Mosiah Ch 9: "king Benjamin had a gift from God" — modern reads "king Mosiah had a gift" (the original Benjamin had already died earlier in the narrative — a plot error that required correction). Read it →

About this edition

The 1830 edition contains 114 chapters, divided by Roman numerals (CHAPTER I, II, III…). The chapter divisions are MUCH longer than what readers know today — 1 Nephi has 7 chapters in 1830, not 22. Alma has 30 chapters in 1830, not 63. The modern verse divisions (1:1, 1:2…) did not exist; they were imposed by LDS apostle Orson Pratt in 1879. The 1830 reads as continuous narrative.

Every book page below maps each 1830 chapter to the corresponding modern chapter:verse range, so you can cross-reference back to the modern edition for comparison.
