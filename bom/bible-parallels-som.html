Sermon on the Mount Parallels

3 Nephi 12–14 ↔ Matthew 5–7

15 parallel passages

When Jesus appears to the Nephites in 3 Nephi after His resurrection, He delivers a sermon that closely parallels the Sermon on the Mount recorded in Matthew 5–7. The parallel is so close that scholars on both sides of the LDS debate agree the texts are linked. The question is whether this represents Christ delivering a similar sermon in two locations (the LDS position), or whether Joseph Smith was incorporating Matthew's text into his dictation (the critical position).
