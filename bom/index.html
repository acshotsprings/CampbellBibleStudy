Suggested cross-reference workflow:
1. Read a Book of Mormon passage in question (e.g., 2 Nephi 12 — which is essentially Isaiah 2 word-for-word).
2. Open the parallel Bible passage in YouVersion (NASB 1995) for comparison.
3. Check Strong's lexicon at Blue Letter Bible if a specific Hebrew/Greek word matters.
4. Take notes in the Quill box on each book page.
