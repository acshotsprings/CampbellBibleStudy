New Testament Echoes

BoM passages that echo NT verses

15 parallel passages

The Book of Mormon contains numerous passages that closely echo New Testament wording — though the Nephites supposedly lost contact with the Old World centuries before the New Testament was written. These parallels are most pronounced where the BoM uses distinctively Pauline language (1 Corinthians 13, Hebrews 11), Johannine themes (John 10's 'other sheep'), or apostolic doctrines that hadn't yet been articulated in OT times.
