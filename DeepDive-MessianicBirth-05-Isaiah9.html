Prophecy 05 · The Child and His Names

A Child Will Be Born to Us

Isaiah 9:6–7

Only chapters after the sign of Immanuel, Isaiah says more about this coming child than perhaps anywhere else in the Old Testament. He is born a child, given as a son, yet the names heaped upon Him belong to God alone. And His government, unlike David's, will never end, tying this promise straight back to the everlasting throne pledged to David.

A note on verse 7
Verse 7 completes the promise — His government and peace increase without end, established "on the throne of David and over his kingdom... forevermore." When we study this together, paste the verbatim NASB 1995 of verse 7 from e-Sword into the study thread so we have both verses side by side.
Why this matters for the birth of Messiah
"A child... a son": A real human birth — yet the titles that follow are divine.
"Mighty God, Eternal Father": No mere king could bear these names; they declare the child's deity.
"On the throne of David... forever": A direct callback to the promise we studied in 2 Samuel 7 — the everlasting throne now has a face.

Questions for our study: How do the four titles fit together — are they one name or four? And how does "the throne of David... forever" connect this child to everything promised before? We let Scripture interpret Scripture (2 Timothy 3:16–17).
